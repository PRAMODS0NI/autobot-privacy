1. Introduction & Purpose

Welcome to Autobot ("we", "our", or "us"). Autobot is an Instagram comment automation tool designed to help creators, businesses, and account holders automatically detect comments on their Instagram posts and deliver immediate, helpful replies or direct message triggers via official Meta/Instagram APIs.

We are committed to transparency and respect for your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, transmission, processing, security, and deletion when you interact with Autobot.

2. Data We Collect

Autobot adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only access and process information that is strictly required to execute automated comment responses:

A. Information Provided via the Instagram Graph API

  • Instagram Account Identifiers: Your Instagram Business or Creator Account ID and public username/handle.
  • Media Information: Instagram post IDs, reels IDs, captions, and permalinks necessary to associate comments with specific posts.
  • Comment Data: Comment text, comment ID, author username, and comment creation timestamp received through Meta Webhooks.

B. Technical & Operational Logs

  • Server Logs: HTTP request metadata, timestamps, response status codes, and error tracebacks strictly used for real-time monitoring and debugging.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We never ask for, collect, or store your Instagram password. Authentication is handled exclusively through official Meta OAuth access tokens.
  • We do not collect financial, payment, or credit card information.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, biometrics, or health data.

3. How Instagram Account Data & Comments Are Processed

Autobot processes comment events in real-time utilizing official Meta Webhook subscriptions:

  1. Real-Time Webhook Reception: When a user posts a comment on your connected Instagram media, Meta sends a webhook payload to our secure endpoint containing the comment ID, comment text, and author ID.
  2. Trigger Matching: Autobot parses the comment text against rule triggers configured by the account owner (for example, matching keywords like "link", "price", or "guide").
  3. Action Dispatch: If a keyword match occurs, Autobot executes the automated action (e.g., publishing a public comment reply or sending an automated direct message) via the Instagram Graph API.
  4. Transient In-Memory Processing: Comment text is evaluated in-flight. We do not maintain perpetual relational databases of comment histories beyond what is technically required for deduplication, rate limit enforcement, and short-term operational error logging.

4. How We Use Information

All information accessed by Autobot is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the automated comment reply functionality.
  • To fulfill requested automation rules configured by the authenticated account holder.
  • To monitor system health, diagnose technical issues, prevent API rate-limit violations, and ensure uptime.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and abuse in compliance with Meta's developer policies.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute your personal or Instagram account data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

5. Data Protection & Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard all information transmitted through Autobot:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between Meta servers, our webhook endpoints, and clients are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Token Security: API tokens and webhook verification secrets are isolated in protected environment variables and are never checked into public version control or exposed on the client side.
  • Access Controls: Server infrastructure access is strictly restricted to authorized maintenance personnel.
  • Minimal Retention: Temporary operational logs are automatically purged on a rolling cycle.

6. Meta Platform & API Compliance

Autobot operates in full accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

We only request the specific API permissions required for comment automation (such as instagram_manage_comments and instagram_basic). Use of information received from Meta APIs adheres to Meta's data handling specifications.

7. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform policies, users have complete control over their data and can request deletion at any time without fees.

How to Request or Complete Data Deletion

You can remove your data and revoke Autobot's access using either of the following methods:

  • Option A: Revoke Access via Instagram / Facebook Settings (Instant)

    You can immediately disconnect Autobot from your Instagram account:

    1. Log in to your Instagram account or connected Facebook Business Page.
    2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Apps and Websites.
    3. Find Autobot under the active apps list.
    4. Click Remove to instantly revoke all permissions and webhook subscriptions.
  • Option B: Submit a Manual Data Deletion Request via Email

    To request complete deletion of any logs or cached identifiers associated with your account:

    1. Send an email to: pramodsoni2290@gmail.com

    Once received, our team will verify your request and permanently delete all records and identifiers associated with your account from our systems within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.
